## Maker's first comment (post as soon as it's live)

> Hi Product Hunt 👋
>
> AI tools like Lovable, Bolt, v0 and Cursor let anyone ship a real app in an afternoon. They also regularly ship the OpenAI or Stripe secret key to the browser, leave Supabase tables readable by anyone, or publish the .env file. The app works perfectly, so nobody notices until a bill arrives or data leaks.
>
> LeakPatrol checks your live app from the outside, the way an attacker would, in about a minute:
> • secret keys in your JavaScript (OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, AWS, Supabase service keys and more)
> • public .env, .git and source maps
> • security headers and HTTPS
> • for Supabase apps, which tables anyone can read
>
> Every finding comes with a plain-English fix and a prompt you can paste straight back into the tool that built your app. It's read-only, never logs in or changes anything, and masks any key it finds.
>
> The free scan is live now. A full report with progress tracking ("proof it's fixed") and a Supabase check that needs no access to your project is coming next.
>
> Timely bonus: Supabase changes a default on 30 Oct, but your existing tables keep their access. We wrote up what to check: https://www.getleakpatrol.com/supabase-october-30-change.html
>
> Would love your feedback, especially false alarms and checks you'd want added. 🙏
